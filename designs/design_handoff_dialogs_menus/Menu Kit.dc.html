Menu kit

Smoosic · sidebar menu buttons + dropdown menu modals · Material Symbols and Bravura
Icons

Two sources, one size class each. .mi is Material Symbols Outlined by ligature name at weight 300 — chrome, files, layout, playback. .bv is Bravura (fonts/Bravura_1.392.woff, copied from your tree) addressed by SMuFL codepoint — anything that names a musical object: clef, note, accidental, rest, accent, dynamic, tuplet numeral.

Behaviour

A menu button opens its dropdown as a modal: same inert layer as the dialogs, so the score is visible and untouchable. The button stays lit while its menu is open, arrow keys walk the items, letters jump by hotkey, Escape closes and returns focus to the button.

Menus

Groups follow the menu modules already in src/ui/menus — file, edit, score, measure, note, text, beams, tuplets, voices, parts, key and time signature, staff modifier, language, help.

1 · Ribbon

Permanent across the top, 48px tall. Icon-only buttons are a 34px square hit box; text buttons add a label and their current value, so key, tempo and time signature read at a glance without opening anything. Groups are separated by a hairline, and part selection sits at the right where it belongs to the score rather than the selection. Container queries shed the text labels, then the part-selection label, as the bar's own width tightens — so it degrades wherever it is placed, not only at the viewport breakpoint.

4 · At 768px

The ribbon keeps every button but drops the text labels — icon-only at 40px, horizontally scrollable if it still overflows. The sidebar collapses to a 52px icon rail — labels drop, the icon carries the meaning, which is why every button has one. The dropdown fills the remaining width and its rows grow to 40px.

Open questions
  • Should submenus open on hover after a delay, or on click only?
  • Hotkeys are shown for every item — do you want them hidden until Alt is held?
  • Ribbon text buttons show the current value (Key D, Tempo = 96). Useful, or too busy for a permanent bar?
  • Bravura glyphs sit optically larger than Material at the same px; I set .bv to 22px against .mi at 20px. Worth a second look on your display.
